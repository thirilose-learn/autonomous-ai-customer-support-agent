# Return Policy

## 1. Overview & General Policy
At our store, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We want you to be completely delighted with your purchase. If you are not satisfied with an item, our return policy enables eligible items to be returned for a replacement or full refund under the conditions specified below.

## 2. Return Window & Eligibility
- **Standard Return Window**: You have **30 calendar days** from the verified delivery date (as recorded by carrier tracking) to initiate a return request.
- **Item Condition Requirements**:
  - The item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same pristine condition in which it was received.
  - All original packaging, user manuals, tags, barcode labels, and included accessories must remain intact and included in the return parcel.
  - Proof of purchase (such as the Order ID or email confirmation) is required for all returns.

## 3. Non-Returnable & Excluded Items
For health, hygiene, safety, and legal reasons, the following categories cannot be returned once delivered:
- Perishable goods (food items, beverages, organic products, plants).
- Intimate apparel, swimwear, undergarments, and personal hygiene products once opened.
- Custom-made, monogrammed, or personalized merchandise.
- Digital downloads, gift cards, and downloadable software products.
- Hazardous materials or items using flammable liquids/gases.

## 4. Return Shipping & Costs
- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items**: If an item arrives damaged, defective, or differs from what was ordered, we provide a prepaid return shipping label at zero cost to you.
- **Change of Mind / Voluntary Returns**: For discretionary returns (e.g., wrong size selected, color preference, or remorse), the customer is responsible for the return shipping postage, which can be deducted from the refund or paid directly at the drop-off courier.

## 5. Return Process & Instructions
1. **Initiate Request**: Navigate to the Orders section of your account or contact our AI Support Agent with your Order Number.
2. **Receive Return Authorization**: Once validated, you will receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and shipping instructions.
3. **Pack Securely**: Place the item in its original box, pack with adequate protection, and affix the RMA label outside the box.
4. **Drop Off**: Hand over the package to an authorized courier drop-off point within 7 business days of authorization.
5. **Inspection**: Upon arrival at our fulfillment center, items undergo inspection within 2 to 3 business days before refund issuance.
