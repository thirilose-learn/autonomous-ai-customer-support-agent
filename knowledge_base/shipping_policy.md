# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Overview & General Policy
Our logistics network works closely with certified regional and national carrier partners to deliver your purchases safely, reliably, and on schedule.

## 2. Processing & Dispatch Times
- **Order Processing**: Orders are verified and released for packing within **24 to 48 hours** (1 to 2 business days) following payment authorization.
- **Operating Hours**: Fulfillment operations run Monday through Friday, excluding national public holidays. Orders placed during weekends are queued for dispatch starting Monday morning.

## 3. Shipping Speeds & Estimated Delivery Timelines
Delivery estimates depend on customer zip code and selected shipping method:
- **Standard Courier Shipping**:
  - Estimated delivery: **5 to 8 business days** for metropolitan areas; up to 12 business days for remote regions.
  - Economical carrier tracking with daily checkpoint scans.
- **Express Priority Shipping**:
  - Estimated delivery: **2 to 3 business days** to major urban hubs.
  - Priority courier dispatch with real-time GPS checkpoint updates.

## 4. Tracking Your Shipment
- Upon carrier handover, a confirmation notification containing your tracking code is generated.
- You can check shipment status in real time by providing your Order ID to our AI Support Agent (e.g., "Where is my order [Order ID]?").
- Tracking updates generally take between 12 and 24 hours to become visible on the courier portal after initial label creation.

## 5. Delayed Deliveries & Weather Exceptions
- High freight seasons (such as Black Friday or holiday peaks) or adverse weather conditions may cause unavoidable courier transit delays.
- If your shipment exceeds the estimated delivery date by more than **5 business days**, contact our support team. We will open an escalation investigation with the carrier.

## 6. Lost, Stolen, or Damaged in Transit
- **Damaged Packages**: Inspect packages immediately upon arrival. If the external carton is visibly crushed or punctured, note the damage with the delivery driver before signing or take clear photos of the carton and contents within 48 hours.
- **Lost in Transit Protocol**: If tracking shows no movement for **10 consecutive business days**, or if the courier confirms the parcel is missing, the shipment is classified as lost. We will issue an immediate replacement shipment at zero cost or issue a 100% full refund.
- **Marked Delivered but Not Received**: If tracking indicates delivery but you have not received the parcel, verify with neighbors and property building managers. If not located within 24 hours, notify our support agent to file a carrier misdelivery investigation.
