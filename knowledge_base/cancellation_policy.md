# Cancellation Policy

## 1. Overview & General Rules
We strive to fulfill customer orders as swiftly as possible. Once an order is placed, our automated logistics system immediately routes items for picking, packing, and dispatch. Because fulfillment happens quickly, cancellation requests are subject to specific timeline constraints.

## 2. Cancellation Window
- **Pre-Dispatch Window**: You can cancel an order for a full 100% refund at any point **before the order has been handed over to the shipping carrier** (i.e. while `order_status` is `created`, `approved`, or `in_preparation`).
- **Post-Dispatch Cutoff**: Once an order status changes to `shipped` or `delivered_to_carrier`, the package has left our fulfillment facility and is in active transit. At that point, the order **cannot be canceled or intercepted in transit**.

## 3. How to Cancel an Order
- **Automated Self-Service**:
  1. Access your account dashboard and select "My Orders".
  2. If the order is eligible, click the "Cancel Order" button.
  3. Select a cancellation reason and confirm.
- **Support Agent Assistance**:
  - You can request cancellation through our AI Support Agent by asking: "Can I cancel order [Order ID]?"
  - The agent will check real-time order status against the database. If eligible, it initiates cancellation immediately.

## 4. What Happens After Dispatch?
If your order has already been dispatched and cannot be canceled:
- You may refuse the delivery when the courier attempts delivery at your doorstep, OR
- You may accept the package and immediately initiate a return under our 30-Day Return Policy.
- Once the returned parcel reaches our fulfillment hub, a full refund for the item value will be processed.

## 5. Merchant Cancellations & Out of Stock
Occasionally, an item may become unavailable due to inventory discrepancies or transit damage prior to dispatch:
- If an item cannot be fulfilled, our operations team will notify you immediately.
- The unavailable item or entire order will be canceled, and a complete 100% refund (including all shipping charges) will be issued automatically within 24 hours.
