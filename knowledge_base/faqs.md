# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## General Ordering & Account

### Q1: How do I track the status of my order?
**A:** You can check the status of your order at any time by asking our AI Support Agent "Where is my order?" along with your 32-character Order ID or demo email. Alternatively, log into your account and view "My Orders" to see current tracking checkpoint scans.

### Q2: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
**A:** If your order status is still `created` or `approved` and has not yet been dispatched to the carrier, contact our support agent immediately to update your shipping address. Once an order is marked `shipped`, the destination address cannot be altered while in transit.

### Q3: Why is my order split into multiple parcels?
**A:** If your order includes multiple items from different seller fulfillment centers, items may ship in separate parcels with individual tracking numbers. You do not incur any additional delivery fees for split shipments.

### Q4: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), digital voucher codes, debit cards, and bank payment slips (Boleto Bancario). Installment payments are available for eligible credit card orders.

### Q5: Is my payment information secure?
**A:** Yes. All transactions are encrypted via industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols and processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways. We never store raw card security CVVs or full credit card numbers on our servers.

## Cancellations & Modifications

### Q6: Can I cancel my order?
**A:** Yes, you can cancel any order free of charge as long as the package has not yet been handed over to the courier (`order_status != 'shipped'`). If the order has already shipped, you may initiate a return once the parcel arrives.

### Q7: How do I cancel an order?
**A:** Simply tell our AI Support Agent: "I want to cancel order [Order ID]" or click "Cancel Order" under the Orders section of your account.

### Q8: Can I add or remove an item from an existing order?
**A:** Once an order is confirmed, individual item quantities cannot be modified. To change items before shipping, cancel the existing order and place a fresh order with your updated items.

## Returns & Exchanges

### Q9: What is the return window?
**A:** You have 30 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery to request a return for eligible items.

### Q10: How do I return a product?
**A:** Contact our support agent or visit your order history to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) label. Securely package the unused item in its original box with all tags attached, affix the label, and drop off at an authorized carrier station.

### Q11: Do I have to pay for return shipping?
**A:** Return shipping is 100% free if the product arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect. For discretionary returns (e.g. change of mind or personal preference), return postage costs are paid by the customer or deducted from the refund.

### Q12: Can I return an item without the original packaging?
**A:** To qualify for a full refund, items must be returned in their original packaging with all included accessories and manuals. Returns missing original packaging may be subject to a restocking deduction or refused.

### Q13: Are any products non-returnable?
**A:** Yes. Perishable goods, opened intimate apparel/swimwear, customized products, and digital gift cards cannot be returned.

## Refunds & Payments

### Q14: How long does a credit card refund take?
**A:** Once a return is inspected and approved, credit card refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank's billing cycle.

### Q15: Can I get my refund in store credit instead?
**A:** Yes. You can choose to receive an instant Store Credit Voucher which includes a 5% bonus credit for future purchases.

### Q16: Why was my shipping charge not refunded?
**A:** Original shipping fees are non-refundable for discretionary returns. If the return was due to merchant fault or damaged goods, shipping fees are refunded in full.

### Q17: Where is my refund?
**A:** If 10 business days have passed since return approval and you have not seen the credit, verify with your card issuer. If still unresolved, provide your Order ID to our support agent for a payment trace reference.

## Shipping & Deliveries

### Q18: What are the standard delivery times?
**A:** Standard shipping takes 5 to 8 business days for metropolitan areas. Express delivery takes 2 to 3 business days.

### Q19: What should I do if tracking shows "Delivered" but I haven't received it?
**A:** First check around your porch, building reception, or with neighbors. If 24 hours pass and the package cannot be located, contact our support agent to open a carrier misdelivery investigation.

### Q20: What happens if my package is lost in transit?
**A:** If tracking shows no activity for 10 consecutive business days, the parcel is declared lost. We will dispatch a free replacement or process a 100% refund immediately.

### Q21: Do you deliver internationally?
**A:** Currently, our fulfillment network services domestic addresses across all states and postal code regions. International shipping is not currently offered.

## Warranty & Repairs

### Q22: What warranty comes with my purchase?
**A:** All consumer electronics, appliances, and durable goods include a 90-day merchant warranty against manufacturing and electrical defects.

### Q23: How do I submit a warranty claim?
**A:** Provide your Order ID, product details, and a clear photo or video of the defect to our AI Support Agent. Our technical team evaluates claims within 2 business days.

### Q24: Does the warranty cover accidental drops or water spills?
**A:** No. The warranty covers factory craftsmanship and internal component failure under normal domestic use. Accidental damage, impact cracks, and liquid immersion are excluded.

### Q25: Can I speak with a human support representative?
**A:** Absolutely. If your issue cannot be resolved by our AI agent, you can request human escalation at any time. Our agent will automatically create a high-priority support ticket and transfer your inquiry to a human specialist.
