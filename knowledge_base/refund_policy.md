# Refund Policy

## 1. Overview & General Policy
This Refund Policy describes how refunds are processed, the timelines for reimbursement, and the acceptable refund methods for purchases made through our e-commerce platform.

## 2. Refund Eligibility Criteria
Refunds are issued under the following circumstances:
- An eligible returned item has been received and approved through physical inspection.
- An order is successfully canceled before shipment.
- An item is confirmed lost in transit by the logistics carrier.
- An item arrives defective or damaged and a replacement is unavailable or declined by the customer.

## 3. Refund Timelines by Payment Method
Once your return inspection or cancellation is approved, our finance department triggers the refund immediately. The time required for the funds to reflect in your account depends on your payment provider:
- **Credit Card / Debit Card**:
  - Processing window: **5 to 7 business days** (some banking institutions may take up to 10 business days or 1 billing cycle).
- **Digital Vouchers & Store Credit**:
  - Processing window: **Instantaneous to 24 hours**. Immediately available for use on your next order.
- **Boleto Bancario / Bank Transfer**:
  - Processing window: **2 to 5 business days** following verification of bank account details.

## 4. Refund Methods & Store Credit Option
- **Original Payment Method**: By default, refunds are returned directly to the payment instrument used during checkout.
- **Store Credit Bonus**: Customers may opt to receive their refund in the form of a Store Credit Voucher. Store credit vouchers are issued instantly and include an additional 5% bonus credit towards future purchases.

## 5. Shipping Fee Deductions
- If the return is due to merchant error, defective merchandise, or transit damage, the entire original payment including freight/shipping charges will be refunded 100%.
- For discretionary returns (e.g. buyer remorse or incorrect sizing), original standard shipping fees and return postage are non-refundable and will be deducted from the total refund amount.

## 6. Delayed or Missing Refunds
If 10 business days have elapsed since receiving your refund confirmation email and the funds have not posted to your bank or card statement:
1. Re-check your online banking or credit card account statement.
2. Contact your bank or credit card issuer, as financial institutions often require internal clearance time before posting pending transactions.
3. If your bank cannot locate the transaction, contact our Support Agent with your Order ID and Refund Reference Number to request an official transaction trace.
