# Warranty Policy

## 1. Overview & General Policy
We stand behind the quality and durability of the products sold on our platform. Products are covered by comprehensive warranty protection against manufacturing defects, electronic malfunctions, and faulty craftsmanship.

## 2. Standard Merchant Warranty Coverage
- **Standard Coverage Period**: All durable goods, consumer electronics, kitchen appliances, and hardware products include a **90-day merchant warranty** commencing from the verified delivery date.
- **What Is Covered**:
  - Mechanical breakdowns or component failures under ordinary, intended domestic usage.
  - Electrical or circuit malfunctions not caused by external power surges or water ingress.
  - Manufacturing defects in materials, stitching, assembly, or factory finishes.
- **What Is NOT Covered**:
  - Accidental drops, physical impact, liquid damage, or cosmetic wear and tear (scratches, dents).
  - Damage arising from misuse, negligence, unauthorized modifications, or improper voltage.
  - Consumable parts and accessories subject to natural depletion (batteries, light bulbs, filters).

## 3. Extended Manufacturer Warranties
- Many premium electronic, appliance, and tech items carry an official **1-year or 2-year manufacturer warranty** directly through the brand.
- For issues occurring after our 90-day merchant period but within the manufacturer's warranty window, our support agent can provide the official manufacturer service center contact details, serial registration guidance, and proof of purchase documents.

## 4. Warranty Claim Process
1. **Report the Issue**: Contact our AI Support Agent with your Order ID, product details, and a clear description of the defect.
2. **Submit Diagnostic Evidence**: Provide clear photos or a short video demonstrating the defect alongside the product serial number.
3. **Technical Assessment**: Our technical warranty team evaluates claims within **2 business days**.
4. **Resolution Options**:
   - **Repair**: Complimentary repair at an authorized regional repair workshop.
   - **Replacement**: An identical replacement product shipped at no expense if repair is not feasible.
   - **Full Refund**: A complete refund issued if a replacement is unavailable and the product is unrepairable.
